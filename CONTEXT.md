# Anvelia Content Language

This context defines the public-facing language for the Anvelia scroll website. It keeps the retreat concept calm, consistent, and easy to expand without making the page feel crowded.

## Language

**Anvelia**:
The English public name for the sanctuary experience. Use it as the primary brand name in headings, navigation, and calls to action.
_Avoid_: Switching between multiple English brand spellings in the same page.

**Anvelia Sanctuary**:
The opening identity for the whole place: a mountain-based wellness sanctuary for rest, healing, food, movement, and slow travel.
_Avoid_: Resort, hotel, clinic, tourist attraction.

**Opening Slide**:
The first public moment should be the quiet logo hero only. The second opening slide should use `anvelia-hero-banner.png` with `Welcome to`, `Anvelia`, and `A warm retreat shaped by forest, mist, and stillness.` in the lower-left.
_Avoid_: Long mission copy, mixed-language competition, or abstract offering labels.

**Healing Pavilion**:
The gathering venue for retreats, circles, and quiet encounters. Use this name for the 4,000 sq ft open pavilion.
_Avoid_: Auditorium, hall, event space.

**Forest Cabins**:
The guest stay concept for the 26 healing cabins with private ritual baths and mountain terraces.
_Avoid_: Rooms, units, chalets.

**Organic Farm**:
The food and cultivation concept behind the farm-to-table experience.
_Avoid_: Agriculture facility, farm project.

**Activities**:
The daily rhythm should read like a quiet schedule with images, time, activity name, and one small description.
_Avoid_: Dense paragraphs, heavy wellness claims, or crowded activity cards.

**Detox & Wellness Program**:
The program page should focus on `2-14 days` and the five duration options.
_Avoid_: Medical treatment language, heavy packages, or too many nested details.

**Visit**:
The final contact section should show only the logo, `Welcome home.`, phone number, and address.
_Avoid_: Booking funnel, lead form, checkout, or redundant reservation copy.
